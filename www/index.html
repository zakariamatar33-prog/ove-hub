Privacy Policy 🔒
What we collect, how we use it, and how to delete your account

Information we collect

When you create an account, we ask for your email address and password to sign you in. To build your profile, we ask for your name, date of birth (to confirm you're 18+ and show your age), gender, a profile photo, and any optional details you choose to add, such as your bio, country, language, relationship status, interests, and other preferences.

How we use it

This information is used to create your profile, show it to other members you may be interested in matching with, let you message other members, and apply the search filters you set. Your date of birth is used only to calculate and display your age and to verify you meet the 18+ requirement — it is never shown publicly. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Who can see your information

Your name, photo, bio, and the other profile details you fill in are visible to other members of the app, since that's how matching and messaging work. Your email address and password are never shown to other members.

Advertising

This app shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob may collect device and usage information to serve and measure ads, in line with Google's own privacy policy.

How to delete your account

You're always in control of your data. To permanently delete your account and profile information, go to Me → Edit Profile and scroll to the bottom, then tap 🗑️ Delete Account and confirm. This immediately and permanently removes your profile from our database and signs you out. This action cannot be undone.

Data retention

We keep your profile information only for as long as your account is active. Once you delete your account, your profile data is removed right away.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, you can reach out to us through the app's support channel.
