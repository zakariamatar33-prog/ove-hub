1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Make Love Hub ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. This App is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: when you register, we collect your email address and a password (stored securely and encrypted by our authentication provider, Google Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password in plain text).

Profile information: your name, date of birth, gender, profile photo, bio, country, spoken language, relationship status, and any other optional details you choose to add to your profile.

Content you create: messages you send to other members, and any photos you upload.

Usage & device data: we and our service providers may automatically collect technical information such as your device type, operating system, app version, general usage patterns (e.g. screens viewed, features used, crash logs), your IP address, and a device identifier. We specifically use your IP address and device identifier for fraud prevention and security — for example, to detect multiple accounts created from the same device or network, which helps us enforce bans and stop abusive users from creating replacement accounts. This data is visible only to our administrators for this purpose; it is never shared with other members, sold, or used for advertising.

Advertising data: the App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID), approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, as described in Google's own Privacy Policy.

Push notification data: the App uses OneSignal to deliver push notifications (such as new-message alerts). OneSignal collects a device identifier and push-subscription token, and we link it to your account so we can notify you of relevant activity, as described in OneSignal's own Privacy Policy.

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone under the age of 18.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: create and display your profile to other members; let you connect with, send Love to, and message other members; verify that you meet the 18+ age requirement (your exact date of birth is never shown publicly — only your calculated age is shown); operate, maintain, and improve the App; detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service; respond to your support requests; and show you relevant, non-personalized or personalized ads, depending on your device settings.

4. How We Share Your Information

With other members: your profile details (name, age, photo, bio, and any other information you choose to add) are visible to other members of the App, since this is necessary for the community and love-sharing service to function. Your email address and password are never shared with or shown to other members.

With service providers: we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database) to store account and profile data, OneSignal to deliver push notifications, and Google AdMob to serve advertising. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security commitments.

For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, investigate fraud, respond to a government request, or protect the safety of our users or the public.

Business transfers: if the App is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction; we will notify you of any such change.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and profile information for as long as your account remains active, so that the App can function and other members can see and interact with your profile. If you delete your account, your profile is hidden from other members immediately, and your personal data is permanently removed from our active database after a 30-day grace period — during which logging back in gives you the option to cancel the deletion and restore your account — except where we are required to retain it longer to investigate an open safety report against your account, or for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Chat messages are automatically deleted 90 days after being sent, or after 60 days when the shorter schedule is enabled. Records of "Give Love" hearts you've received are automatically deleted after 30 days. Photos sent in chat may also be automatically deleted after 40 days to conserve storage space, when this option is enabled. Accounts with no login activity for 9 months or more are automatically removed. If you report a message or photo, the reported content is kept with your report regardless of these schedules, so the report remains valid even after the original content is deleted.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and Google Firebase's built-in security infrastructure — to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You can review and update most of your profile information at any time from Me → Edit Profile. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data — such as the right to access, correct, export, or request deletion of your data, or to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11.

You can also limit ad personalization at any time from your device's system settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android), which controls how AdMob and other advertisers use your advertising identifier.

8. How to Delete Your Account

You are always in control of your data. To delete your account, go to Me → Edit Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap 🗑️ Delete Account, then confirm. Your profile is hidden from other members and you are signed out immediately. If you log back in within the next 30 days, you'll be asked whether you'd like to cancel the deletion and restore your account; if you don't log back in (or decline restoring it), your account and associated data are permanently and irreversibly erased once the 30 days are up — except where there is an open safety report against your account, in which case erasure is deferred until that investigation is resolved.
